Common fixes

Basic troubleshooting

ScamShield does not detect Marketplace

Refresh the Facebook page, reopen the conversation, confirm ScamShield is enabled, and reopen the extension popup.

Protection is limited or unavailable

Changes to Facebook or Messenger interfaces may temporarily affect detection in supported contexts. Refresh the page and try again later if the issue continues.

The extension popup does not respond

Reload the page, restart the browser, or disable and re-enable the extension if appropriate.

A result overlay remains visible

Temporary result overlays can be manually dismissed and are also designed to auto-dismiss.
