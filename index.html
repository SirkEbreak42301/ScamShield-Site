Two clear assessments

What ScamShield does

ScamShield evaluates visible, supported buyer inquiries and presents two distinct views. Scam Risk and Bot Likelihood are separate assessments.

01

Scam Risk

Looks for potentially suspicious message characteristics and summarizes the result with:

  • Risk level
  • Score
  • Confidence
  • Reasons and signals
02

Bot Likelihood

Separately evaluates signs that an inquiry may be automated or template-generated, helping you interpret the conversation before you reply.

Possible automation does not, by itself, determine Scam Risk.

A simple review flow

How it works

  1. 1

    Open a conversation

    Open a supported Marketplace buyer conversation in your browser.

  2. 2

    Analyze visible messages

    ScamShield analyzes supported incoming messages currently visible in the conversation.

  3. 3

    Review before responding

    Consider Scam Risk, Bot Likelihood, confidence, and reasons as you decide what to do next.

Privacy by design

Your messages are not a product

ScamShield is local-first. Raw buyer-message text is analyzed in memory, while behavioral history uses bounded fingerprints and timestamps rather than retaining raw buyer messages. Default operation requires no telemetry or cloud AI.
