ScamShield Privacy Policy

The approved privacy policy governing ScamShield.

ScamShield processes visible Facebook Marketplace and Messenger inquiry text locally in the browser. Normal operation makes no external network requests and sends no messages, feedback, or trust decisions to a server.

Local extension storage may contain settings, bounded message fingerprints and timestamps for behavior history, local allow/block and trust decisions, false-positive feedback metadata, and diagnostic counters. Raw buyer-message text is used temporarily for analysis and is not retained in behavior history. Users can clear feedback, lists, settings, or all local data in Settings.

AI assistance is future-facing only. The default provider is local/no-op; no external AI provider or message transmission is enabled.
